# Recurring operational tasks

These recurring tasks were created with explicit approval and follow the weekly review cadence during active outreach and campaign windows.

## Weekly tasks

### 1. Program officer outreach review
- Cadence: Every Monday, 9:00 AM
- Goal: Review new foundation and program officer prospects, update outreach stage, and confirm follow-up deadlines.
- Output: Refresh `grants/prospects.json` and prepare any outreach escalations or next steps.

### 2. Grants pipeline follow-up
- Cadence: Every Wednesday, 2:00 PM
- Goal: Check outstanding LOI and email follow-ups, send reminders, and record replies.
- Output: Update status notes, contact dates, and any next-touch action items.

### 3. Donor portal and payment review
- Cadence: Every Thursday, 4:00 PM
- Goal: Monitor donor funnel health, conversion rate, and any issues in the donation form or Stripe flow.
- Output: Log bugs, shipping blockers, and follow-up improvements for the portal.

### 4. Donation receipt and stewardship check
- Cadence: Every Friday, 11:00 AM
- Goal: Confirm receipt emails, gratitude notes, and donor stewardship actions are firing correctly.
- Output: Review any failed sends, refund issues, or donor support follow-ups.

## Operating notes
- This cadence is active during active outreach/campaign periods.
- If outreach slows or campaign windows close, the team can move back to a lighter review rhythm by agreement.
- Any task can be adjusted or paused without creating overlapping recurring work.
